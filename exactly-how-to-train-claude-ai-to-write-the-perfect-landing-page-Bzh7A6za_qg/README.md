## 🎯 Framework Overview

The 10-section psychological sequence:
1. **Hero Section** - 5-second conversion test with eyebrow copy, emotional headline, CTA
2. **Quick Outcomes** - Three checkmark results (3-4 words each)
3. **Problem Agitation** - Main pain point with specific symptoms
4. **Solution Introduction** - Establish mentor role with real photo
5. **Benefits Section** - Three main benefits (not features) with visuals
6. **Testimonials** - Three specific testimonials addressing objections
7. **3-Step Process** - Their action → Your process → Their outcome
8. **Flex Section** - "10 Reasons Why" list or comparison chart
9. **Features Section** - 10-20 logical features for analytical buyers
10. **FAQ Section** - 5-6 questions addressing objections
11. **Final CTA** - Repeat main call-to-action with dream outcome focus
