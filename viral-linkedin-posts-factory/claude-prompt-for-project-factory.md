# Research Request: Top 14 Viral LinkedIn Post Formats

## Objective

Conduct comprehensive research to identify, analyze, and document the top 14 viral post formats that consistently perform on LinkedIn. For each format, provide the underlying mechanics (why it works), structural templates (how to write it), real examples, common mistakes, and variations.

## Context & Purpose

This research serves dual purposes:
1. **Content Creation** — Deliver ready-to-adapt templates and examples for immediate implementation
2. **Technical Implementation** — Explain the underlying psychology and mechanics so I can engineer my own variations

Understanding both the "what" and the "why" enables sustainable content creation rather than blind template copying.

## Success Criteria

This research succeeds if it:

- Maps the complete landscape of proven viral LinkedIn post formats (14 formats)
- Provides actionable writing guidance for each format (not just descriptions)
- Includes structural templates that can be immediately adapted
- Explains why each format triggers engagement (psychological/algorithmic mechanics)
- Shows real examples as evidence of effectiveness
- Identifies common mistakes and how to avoid them
- Rates confidence level (high/medium/low) for each format's proven effectiveness

## Source Requirements

**Prioritize:**
- Industry and practitioner sources (LinkedIn creators, marketing experts, viral post analysts)
- Primary sources (actual viral posts as examples, creator case studies, LinkedIn's own creator resources)
- Expert practitioners with demonstrated results (e.g., Justin Welsh, Jasmin Alić, Lara Acosta, and similar LinkedIn-focused creators)

**Verification approach:**
- Cross-reference format recommendations across multiple practitioner sources
- Use actual viral post examples as evidence
- Note when sources disagree on a format's effectiveness

## Time Focus

**Past 12 months** — LinkedIn's algorithm and user behavior have evolved significantly. Focus on what's working now (2024-2025), filtering out outdated tactics while capturing formats with proven recent performance.

## Research Approach

**Depth over breadth** — For each of the 14 formats, provide thorough analysis:

1. **Format name and category**
2. **Why it works** (psychological triggers, algorithm signals, reader behavior)
3. **Structural template** (the actual framework/skeleton)
4. **Real example(s)** demonstrating the format in action
5. **How to write it** (step-by-step implementation guidance)
6. **Common mistakes** and how to avoid them
7. **Variations** within the format type
8. **Confidence rating** (high/medium/low) based on evidence strength

## Output Format

**Thematic organization** — Group the 14 formats by category type:

- Contrarian/hot take formats
- Story-based formats
- List and framework formats
- Question and engagement formats
- Authority and expertise formats
- (Other categories as research reveals)

Within each category, present formats with full depth analysis per the research approach above.

## Scope Boundaries

**Include:**
- Organic reach strategies only
- Writing techniques and structural formats
- Formats that work across industries (not niche-specific)
- Psychology of why formats trigger engagement
- Algorithm-friendly structural elements

**Exclude:**
- Paid amplification tactics
- Engagement pod strategies
- Clickbait or controversy-baiting tactics
- Grey-hat growth hacks
- Platform manipulation techniques
- Industry-specific formats that don't transfer

## Uncertainty Handling

Rate each format with confidence levels:

- **High confidence** — Multiple sources confirm, abundant viral examples, consistent practitioner consensus
- **Medium confidence** — Some evidence of effectiveness, may be context-dependent, emerging format
- **Low confidence** — Limited evidence, niche success, or conflicting practitioner opinions

When sources conflict on a format's effectiveness, note the disagreement and explain the conditions under which each perspective applies.

## Iteration Guidance

Proceed autonomously with best interpretation. Deliver complete research in one comprehensive pass. Make reasonable assumptions where needed, noting any significant assumptions made.
