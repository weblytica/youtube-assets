- Focus on [Personality Type] specific challenges, traits, and behavioral patterns
- Include both strengths and vulnerabilities related to [Document Title]

- Examine compatibility/effectiveness factors with different approaches or contexts

3. Decision-making processes in [Document Title] contexts

6. Long-term success strategies and maintenance approaches

Output Requirements: Synthesize findings into actionable insights for both [Personality Type] individuals and those who interact with them in [Document Title] contexts, focusing on practical improvement strategies rather than theoretical frameworks.
